# YUKTI Engineering & Projects - Design Brief

## Concept

Precision Trace. The website should feel like a senior engineering consultancy: calm, exact, visually assured, and easy to trust. The visual system combines deep navy technical fields, warm metallic-gold accents, disciplined linework, and large editorial photography. It interprets the supplied brochure identity without imitating a generic corporate template.

## Delivery tier

Editorial, cinema-lite. Strong photography, refined typography, responsive compositions, and light CSS interaction. No generated film or video is required.

Animation mode: non-animated - user picked Non-animated after animated generation was unavailable

## Brand system

- Primary navy: #0F1C32
- Deep navy: #081322
- Engineering gold: #D9A655
- Dark gold: #9D6C29
- Paper: #F5F2EB
- White: #FFFFFF
- Graphite: #273445
- Display type: Outfit, with a system sans fallback
- Technical labels: IBM Plex Mono, with a monospace fallback
- Voice: concise, credible, practical, technically informed

## Visual direction

- Image-first hero with a full-width multidisciplinary engineering scene.
- Fine technical rules, measured numbering, small coordinate-like labels, and gold line accents.
- Layouts use off-grid alignment and deliberate whitespace rather than repeated card grids.
- White and paper sections alternate with full-bleed navy bands.
- Supplied YUKTI logo remains the primary identity. The transparent mark is used in compact spaces and the full lockup is used where legibility permits.
- Generated stills cover multidisciplinary, electrical, civil/structural, and mechanical engineering. Higgsfield generation was unavailable, so built-in still image generation was used.

## Page and section plan

### Home

1. Image-first hero: headline, positioning, and two bespoke calls to action over a full-width engineering scene.
2. Positioning manifesto: a large editorial statement and compact proof points.
3. Services: three horizontal discipline bands plus a specialist-services rail.
4. Method: Understand, Engineer, Deliver as a precision process line.
5. Multidisciplinary feature: staggered image crops showing electrical, structural, and mechanical work.
6. Sectors: asymmetric matrix for seven operating contexts.
7. Why YUKTI: full navy manifesto band with six differentiators.
8. Contact bay: direct phone, email, and enquiry action.

### Supporting pages

- About: company position, commitment, vision, mission, values, and philosophy.
- Services: overview plus seven detailed service routes.
- Sectors: operating contexts and engagement model.
- Contact: accessible enquiry form using the visitor's email application, plus direct phone and email links.
- Privacy Policy and Terms: concise, appropriate informational pages.
- Custom not-found state integrated with the same navigation and visual language.

## Responsive behavior

- Wide compositions collapse to a single editorial column below tablet width.
- Service bands, sector matrix, image crops, and process line become stacked modules on mobile.
- Navigation changes to an accessible native disclosure menu.
- Headlines use fluid type sizing; spacing tightens without reducing readability.
- Calls to action become full-width when the viewport is narrow.
- Images maintain intentional focal crops with explicit aspect ratios.

## Interaction and accessibility

- Motion is limited to subtle hover, focus, and navigation transitions.
- Reduced-motion preferences remove transitions and transforms.
- Keyboard-visible focus states, semantic landmarks, descriptive alt text, and one H1 per page.
- Contrast targets WCAG AA or better.
- The contact form stores no data and clearly states that submission opens the visitor's email application.
